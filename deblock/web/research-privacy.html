Terms of use and privacy policy

Version 3

Accepting these terms allows deblock to store short activity summaries for future personalization. You keep ownership of your writing. Sharing your words with the researcher requires a separate choice. A random browser ID links your sessions. These records are pseudonymous: the ID can link visits, even though it contains no name, email or account ID. People sharing one browser can share the same ID.

A summary records the mode, approximate word count and duration, session outcome, app version and session dates. In a fresh Signifier Chain it also records whether you started within two minutes when that can be observed. You may choose a usefulness rating after Done. When you bookmark an aphorism after accepting these terms, we also collect its catalog ID under your browser ID; removing the bookmark removes that ID from collection. Bookmarks saved before acceptance are not automatically collected. This does not share your response to the aphorism. The summary excludes your words, titles, prompt responses, edits, exact word timings and Blackout selections.

You can review these terms or delete your collected data from the privacy link in the footer. After deletion, collecting new summaries requires accepting the terms again. Complete writing is off by default. Private library saves work independently. An earlier automatic setting does not count as accepting these terms. If you previously chose detailed behavioral research, its separate control lets you stop it and erase those measurements.

If you share writing, the researcher may personally read and analyze your complete text, including drafts, titles, story outlines, prompt references and Blackout source and selections. Earlier checkpoints may contain text you subsequently edit. Text can identify you if you include personal details. Existing drafts require a separate choice to include them.

Each submission includes a study ID, session ID, exercise, app version and session dates. Authorized submissions are temporarily queued in this browser while delivery is pending and stored in the project's private Railway PostgreSQL database. Access is limited to authorized project members. No public writing lookup is provided.

Study records are kept until you remove a choice or delete your data. Removing a choice erases its writing or measurements from the live database. Session headers and consent history stay until full deletion. Backup copies expire on the project's configured backup schedule. The researcher must apply deletions to any research exports.

Open privacy in the footer to withdraw writing permission or delete your data. A private recovery key lets you do this from another browser. Keep the key before clearing storage. Without the key or original browser, deblock cannot look up your study ID by name or email.
