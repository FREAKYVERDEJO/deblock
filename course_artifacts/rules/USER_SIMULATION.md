# User-simulation rules

1. Treat personas as assumptions, not real people or proof of demand.
2. Critique the concrete first-use journey: open, understand privacy, begin,
   continue, stop, and optionally save.
3. For each finding, name the persona, screen or step, heuristic, severity, and
   the directive invariant that permits or rejects the change.
4. Accept at most five changes per wave. Defer or reject additions that do not
   improve the selected journey.
5. A simulated finding never substitutes for interviews or a consent-based pilot.
