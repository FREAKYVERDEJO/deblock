# deblock niche brief

**Decision date:** 2026-11-04
**Decision owner:** Project creator
**Status:** First research brief; not market validation

## Product

deblock is a private, low-pressure writing tool that helps a person begin through
short language-association exercises. The first exercise, *The Signifier Chain*,
asks the person to follow their own associations. It does not generate writing,
interpret it, diagnose the writer, or require a productivity target.

## Candidate niches

| Candidate | Who | Job to be done | Why it may fit | What is still unknown |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| A | Adult creative writers who currently feel blocked | Begin a poem, story, journal entry, or scene without pressure to make it good | Directly matches the product problem and current prototype | Whether the exercise gets them to start, and what privacy explanation they need |
| B | Poetry writers who use language play as a practice | Generate surprising word connections before drafting | The association chain may suit their existing process | Whether it is distinctive enough from notebooks, prompts, and freewriting |
| C | Fiction writers stuck at the opening of a scene | Move from an empty page to material they can develop | The product makes a small first action clear | Whether a word chain feels useful for scene-level work |
| D | Writing students who dislike graded prompts | Start a private exploratory writing exercise | Non-evaluative framing may reduce pressure | Whether an instructor context changes the privacy or sharing expectation |

## Decision

Test **Candidate A** first: adults who self-identify as currently blocked and
write creatively. It is the smallest segment that directly matches the stated
problem. Candidates B-D remain hypotheses, not separate products.

## Evidence and limits

- The project creator's first-person experience establishes a real starting
  problem, but does not represent a market.
- The product specification cites general writing and creativity sources, but
  none establishes demand or effectiveness for deblock.
- Existing products reviewed in the specification suggest a positioning contrast:
  deblock is private and non-punitive rather than streak- or timer-driven.
- No market-size, willingness-to-pay, competitor-pricing, or pilot data has been
  collected. This brief deliberately contains no estimated market numbers.

## What could change the decision

Move away from Candidate A, or revise the product, if interviews or a pilot show
that participants:

1. do not understand what happens to their writing;
2. experience the association exercise as more pressure rather than less;
3. do not begin writing more readily than with a blank page; or
4. consistently ask for a materially different first action.

## Next evidence

1. Interview 5-8 adults in Candidate A about a recent blocked-writing moment,
   tools they tried, and their privacy expectations. Do not ask whether they
   "would use" deblock as the main question.
2. Run a small, consent-based comparison of a blank page and The Signifier Chain.
   Measure only whether and when writing begins; do not collect private text for
   the metric.
3. Record sources and unknowns in [fact_ledger.md](fact_ledger.md), then update
   this brief after real evidence is available.
