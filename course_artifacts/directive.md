# deblock directive

deblock exists to help people begin writing and explore their own relationship to
language. It succeeds when a writer can start and find the process useful, not
when their output is polished, productive, or psychologically revealing.

## Invariants

1. The user writes; the product never replaces their voice.
2. Exercises are invitations, not tests of talent, mental health, or worth.
3. Personal writing is private by default; sharing requires a deliberate user
   action and must be explained before writing begins.
4. The product never diagnoses, interprets, or claims to uncover unconscious
   truth from writing.
5. A writer may pause, stop, skip reflection, or change direction without
   punishment or implied failure.

Only the project owner may change these invariants. When a proposed change
conflicts with one, reject it and cite the invariant number.
