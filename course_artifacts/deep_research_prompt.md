# Research prompt: blocked creative writers' first action

Research one uncertainty: **What do adult creative writers who feel blocked do
at the moment they want to begin, what have they already tried, and what makes a
private language-association exercise feel helpful or pressuring?**

Use interviews and publicly available writing-community discussions only as
context. Keep direct quotes attributable to a source and date. Separate:

1. direct observations;
2. a participant's own report;
3. reviewer interpretation; and
4. hypotheses for deblock.

Do not infer mental health, diagnose writer's block, or treat online discussion
volume as prevalence. Do not collect private writing. For every numeric claim,
return its source, date, population, method, and limitation.

The output should answer:

- Which part of beginning is difficult: choosing a subject, judging the first
  sentence, continuing, or something else?
- What prompts, timers, notebooks, or routines have they tried?
- What would make the privacy explanation trustworthy?
- Does a word-association chain feel like an invitation, a constraint, or both?
- What result would tell us to abandon or redesign this first exercise?

Write findings into `fact_ledger.md` and revise `niche_brief.md`; do not turn
research into product features without an owner decision.
