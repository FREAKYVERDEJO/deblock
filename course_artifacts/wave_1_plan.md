# Wave 1: first-use critique plan

**Status:** Planned. No simulated or real-user findings have been recorded.

## Question

Can a first-time writer understand privacy, start an association chain, and stop
without pressure?

## Materials

- Local prototype at its exercise screen.
- `directive.md`.
- The three assumption-based entries in `personas.yaml`.

## Walkthrough for each persona

1. Open the exercise and identify what happens to writing before entering text.
2. Enter a starting word and a second association.
3. Find instructions without losing the ability to continue.
4. Stop or finish the session.
5. Identify whether saving is local, public, or unclear.

## Triage rule

Accept only changes that improve a writer's understanding, authorship, privacy,
or freedom to stop. Reject a change if it conflicts with an invariant in
`directive.md`. After the wave, replace the placeholder row with actual findings
and write down accepted, deferred, and rejected counts.
